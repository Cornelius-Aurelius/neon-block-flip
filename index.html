How to play

Each move tips the prism onto its side or rolls it upright. Occupy solid floor tiles only, and land upright in the glowing exit hole to finish the chamber.

  1. Move: Arrow keys, WASD, touch direction buttons or swipe on the board.
  2. Void: The prism falls if any part goes beyond the platform. The level restarts.
  3. Fragile (pink with crack): Supports a prism lying down, but breaks under an upright prism.
  4. Switch (amber ⚡): Stand upright to toggle bridges on or off.
  5. Bridge (purple ⬡): Crossable only when a switch has activated it.
  6. Portals (magenta ⇄): Standing upright on one sends the prism to the other.
  7. Undo: Press Z or Undo to reverse a valid move. R restarts the chamber.
  8. Hint (H): Shows one safe move from your current position. Perfect 3-star runs use no hints.
  9. Mastery: Earn 1–3 stars per chamber. Finish efficiently without hints or falls for 3 stars. Change the colour theme anytime.

Complete all 33 unique, verified-solvable chambers. Aim to finish at or under the target number of moves.
